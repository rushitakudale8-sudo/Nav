    Nursing & Patient Care Services

    Nurses | Caregivers | Patient Care Assistants | Ward Attendants

    Nurses

    Nursing support for routine patient observation, medication reminders as directed by a clinician, and day-to-day care tasks. Assignments are discussed according to the requesting facility or household need.

    Caregivers

    Caregivers who assist with daily living activities such as mobility support, hygiene help, meal assistance and companionship in home or facility settings.

    Patient Care Assistants

    Assistants who support feeding, positioning, basic comfort care and coordination with family members or facility staff during recovery or long-term care.

    Ward Attendants

    Ward attendants for housekeeping support, patient movement assistance and day-to-day ward duties in hospitals, clinics and nursing facilities.

    Service availability is discussed case by case. Descriptions are informational and do not claim specific licences, specialisations or clinical outcomes. Product information only — not medical advice.
